## What the Resy Credit is

American Express pays a statement credit when you use an enrolled, eligible card at a qualifying Resy restaurant. On the Platinum Card it is up to **$100 per calendar quarter, $400 a year**; on the Gold Card it is **up to $100 a year**. Amex describes the network as **over 10,000 qualifying U.S. Resy restaurants**. You must enroll in the benefit first, add the card to your Resy profile, and pay with that card. Credits can take up to eight weeks to post. Restaurant software charges and Amex Resy-branded gift cards are excluded.

The important detail: **not every restaurant on Resy qualifies.** Eligibility is per venue and changes over time, which is what this list was built to pin down.

## How this list was built

The **Resy Credit** filter was applied at every discovery center across the nine-county Bay Area, using Resy's 20-mile map-search radius, and each center was paginated to its final page. Venue identities were deduplicated across overlapping search radii and assigned once to a region.

Every one of the 248 restaurants below was then opened on its **own Resy venue page**, which displayed the exact text **“Qualifies for Resy Credit.”** A filter hit alone was not treated as proof. Each was cross-checked against its live Google Maps listing for rating, review count, address, and category, and up to three visible individual reviews were read. Review quotes are genuine excerpts capped at about 20 words. Where Google data was missing, the table says so rather than guessing.

## The 75 Tock-redirect venues — and what the policy actually says

These venues appeared under the Resy Credit filter, but clicking them redirected straight to Tock, so there was no individual Resy venue page on which to read the badge. That is why they are reported separately and are **not** included in the verified count above. It is a limit of the verification method, not a verdict on eligibility.

Here is what changed, and why these are worth a second look:

- Amex owns both Resy and Tock. Tock venues became bookable through Resy on **August 11, 2026**, and the Resy Credit began applying to Tock venues on **September 15, 2026** — four days before this research was done.
- **Hundreds of U.S. Tock venues** were made Resy Credit-eligible on that date, with more added on a rolling basis. This skews heavily toward fine dining, tasting menus, and wineries — exactly the category that dominates the list below.
- Appearing on Resy does **not** by itself mean a Tock venue qualifies. The deciding signal is the same **“Qualifies for Resy Credit”** banner, and for participating Tock venues that banner is shown **on the Tock booking page as well as on Resy**.
- The mechanics are otherwise unchanged: enroll in the benefit, pay with the eligible Amex card. No reservation is strictly required.
- One genuinely open question: Tock is built around **prepaid and ticketed** experiences, and Amex has not spelled out how prepaid deposits and ticketed tasting menus are treated. If a venue charges you weeks ahead through Tock, do not assume the credit behaves the same way as a card swipe at the table.

**Practical takeaway:** treat this section as a to-check list, not a rejection list. Open the Tock page, look for the banner, and confirm before you count on the credit.

## Caveats

1. **Eligibility is a live state.** Every restaurant above displayed the qualification text on September 19, 2026. Venues join and leave the program at any time — recheck the badge before you pay.
2. **Card terms still apply.** Enroll first, pay with the enrolled eligible American Express card, and mind the benefit period and limit. Credits can take up to eight weeks to post.
3. **Google ratings are snapshots.** Counts and visible review ordering change constantly.
4. **Some Google listings are shared.** Hotel and restaurant hybrids (River's End, Nick's Cove, Calistoga Inn) show whole-property ratings, and Chez Panisse's café and downstairs restaurant share one listing, so their ratings are not separable.
5. **Review synthesis is deliberately conservative.** Where three visible reviews did not repeat a caution, the table says so instead of inventing one.
6. **Resy price tiers are coarse.** A `$` or `$$` symbol does not cap the final bill — especially for tasting menus, sushi, cocktails, tax, and tip.
7. **This is the nine-county Bay Area only**, and complete within the boundaries described above.
